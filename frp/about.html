Meet the owner

Brian May

I began my career working at a highly reputable local Audi/VW repair shop, when I was only qualified to mop floors and wash cars. After a few months of proving my work ethic, I put the mop down and picked up some tools to begin my apprenticeship. I was fortunate to be trained by some of the best in the business, and quickly excelled in my trade.

Years later, the shop was sold and I went to work for a local Audi dealership. During this time I received years of professional training and experience, and I’m certified at the highest possible level known as “Master Guild.”

After over a decade of dealership employment, I decided to take my knowledge and experience to Agoura Hills, CA, where I serve the Audi/VW owners in my community. Here at Four Rings Performance, you’ll have the experience of communicating directly and personally with a skilled, factory-certified technician, along with receiving the highest quality of workmanship. I build relationships with clients based on trust and respect, and hope to chat with you soon.
