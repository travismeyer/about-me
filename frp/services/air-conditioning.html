Air Conditioning Repairs

In sunny California your AC is constantly at work. It needs to be regularly serviced for maximum performance, or repaired before you're stuck in a car blowing hot air. When your Audi, Volkswagen, BMW, Mini Cooper, Mercedes or Porsche needs AC repairs, trust Four Rings Performance for the best care possible.

Call (818) 889-1245 Read 5-star reviews

We're certified mechanics

An AC system is complex — switches, sensors, relays and control modules — and you'll want a trained technician on it. We use only high-quality OEM parts, check coolant levels, inspect for a defective control valve or thermostat issue, and make sure the heater core isn't plugged. Quality work, without dealership pricing.

Why is my AC not blowing cold air?

  • Noise when the AC is turned on
  • Warm air coming from the vents
  • Little or no cool air coming from the vents
  • Overheating
  • Engine bogs down when using the AC
  • Interior of the car develops a foul odor

Our services

  • Topping up low freon levels
  • Fixing leaks from a bad hose or seal
  • Defective control valve replacement or repair
  • Fixing a thermostat stuck open
  • Cleaning a plugged heater core
  • Fixing condenser, evaporator or blower motor issues
  • Fixing compressor or compressor clutch issues
